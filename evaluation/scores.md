## Matching and adjudication

Each RA evidence span is located in its abstract by exact match, then with whitespace and quotes normalised; an ellipsis at the start or end of a span is trimmed, and a span joined by an ellipsis is split into pieces, located only if every piece is found; spans still not found are left out. A model and a RA component are paired automatically if their evidence spans overlap by at least 50% of the shorter span's length (for a split span, summed over its pieces), each component pairing at most once, largest overlap first. For adjudication, two kinds of pair were reviewed by hand: every automatic pair whose types differ, and, for components left unpaired, each RA component's most similar unpaired model component in the same abstract by label (sentence-embedding cosine similarity of at least 0.5). Automatic pairs judged not the same component are unpaired; label candidates judged the same component are paired. Decisions are in `adjudication_decisions.csv` (pair ids and Y/N only).
