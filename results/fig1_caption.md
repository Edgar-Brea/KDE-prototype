**Figure 1. Composition of knowledge components by type, 2002–2026.** Each band is one type's share of all components coded in abstracts published that year; the bands always sum to 100%. From bottom to top: phenomenon, principle, means, purpose. Only papers found by the `crispr` search strand are shown: it is the only search that runs the whole timeline; the antecedent searches stop between 2005 and 2011. All strands stay coded and released with their `strand` column. Shares are smoothed with a 3-year centred window: component counts are pooled across the 3 years and shares taken from the pooled counts (at the first and last year, and next to years with no abstracts, the window holds only the years that have them). Band edges are drawn with a shape-preserving spline between years. Years with fewer than 30 abstracts are faded. The strip below shows the number of abstracts per year on a log scale. n = 69,571 abstracts, 836,857 components; abstracts over 5,000 characters (probably body text) and papers without a publication year are excluded.

Faded years (7): 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008.

Years with no abstracts inside the range (0): none.
